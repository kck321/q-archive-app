## Method and limits

- Segmentation splits a line on `?`/`!`/`.` only where followed by whitespace and a capital, digit or quote — so `twitter.com`, `9.11` and `U.S.` are not split.
- `>>NNNNNNN` pointer lines and everything in `quotedPosts` are excluded: they are not Q-authored.
- A question mark gives HIGH confidence. An interrogative or auxiliary lead without one gives MEDIUM (ends `.`/`!`) or LOW (no terminal punctuation).
- `Define X.` counts as a question (it asks for information); `Follow the money.` does not (it asks for an action).
- Elliptical noun phrases count only with a `?`. Without one they are treated as statements, which is a deliberate under-call — they are the largest remaining judgement area.
- Every MEDIUM/LOW unit is in the review queue rather than silently decided.
