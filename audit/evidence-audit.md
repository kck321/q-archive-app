## Two decisions before this can be certified


### 1. Do internal `>>` pointers belong in this section?

There are **1,648** pointers to other drops across 1,591 posts. They are genuinely references — Q citing his own earlier material — but they are not *external* source material, and the section definition reads "material Q presents or points readers toward as supporting information or source material". Counting them would make Q's self-citation the largest single category after links.

They are recorded separately above so the call is yours: include, exclude, or show as their own sub-section.


### 2. Counting occurrences vs distinct sources

Links are counted as **occurrences** (2,666) with distinct URLs reported alongside (2,521), matching the rule used for Questions, Directives and Claims. The same article cited in six drops is six occurrences of one source — collapsing them would hide how often Q returned to it. Confirm that reading for this section too.
