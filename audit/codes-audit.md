# Q Drops — Codes & Brackets audit (v1, candidate)

A **notation** layer, not a semantic class. Q’s exact characters are preserved, and a detected pattern never implies its meaning is known. **No production write, no deploy.**


> Q Drops preserves coded language exactly as written. A detected code or bracket pattern does not imply that its meaning is known. Interpretations are shown only when supported by context or a reviewed resolution, and unresolved items can be submitted to the Resolution Center.

## What is deliberately NOT here

| Excluded | Distinct | Occurrences | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brackets around an ordinary word | 551 | 769 | `[raid]`, `[now]`, `[children]` — Q marking a word for attention, which is **Emphasis** |
| Date fragments | 211 | 405 | `08/09`, `03/31` are timestamps Q cited, not ciphers |

ALL CAPS on its own is also excluded: Q writes in caps constantly, and caps become notation only with structure — an underscore, a digit, a bracket.
