# Q Drops — Emphasis audit (v1, candidate)

A presentation layer: **how** Q draws attention, not what a post is about and not whether the highlighted material is a code. **No production write, no deploy.**


## The rule that keeps capitals from swallowing the section

Q writes in capitals constantly, so a caps detector alone would tag most of the corpus. **Emphasis is CONTRAST**: a capitalised word inside a lowercase line is a highlight; a caps line inside an all-caps post is Q’s baseline register. Lines and posts at 60% capitals or more are excluded on that basis alone.


And nothing is recorded that a reader cannot see. "Cryptic messaging" was the old extractor’s most common label at 401, and it is an interpretation — there is no device on the page to point at. Only brackets, repetitions, punctuation runs and spacing count.

### By type

| Type | Occurrences | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Bracket emphasis | 716 | A word or phrase set in brackets to mark it out — [raid], [now], [children]. |
| Capitals | 2,418 | Capitalised words that contrast with the surrounding lowercase text. |
| Repeated word or phrase | 118 | The same word or phrase repeated in one drop for force. |
| Repeated question | 95 | The same question asked more than once in a drop. |
| Repeated directive | 11 | The same instruction given more than once in a drop. |
| Punctuation intensity | 157 | Runs of punctuation — ???, !!!, ?!?! — beyond ordinary sentence marking. |
| Deliberate spacing | 1 | Spacing or fragmentation used to slow a reader down: S P A C E D, or one word per line. |
| Quoted word | 624 | A single word in quotation marks to mark it as loaded or ironic. |
| Parallel phrasing | 1,111 | A repeated rhetorical frame across consecutive lines — a cascade of three or more, a mirrored construction, or a frame of several words repeated. A shared first word alone is not enough. |

## Excluded, and why

| Excluded | Count | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Already a certified code | 555 | belongs to Codes & Brackets |
| Caps that are Q’s baseline register | 7,839 | a caps line inside an all-caps post is not a highlight |
| Quoted source lines | 932 | repetition in pasted material is the source repeating itself |
| Ordinary acronyms | 997 | FBI and DOJ are names, not highlights |
| Words Q always capitalises | 1,239 | DECLAS and CLAS have no lowercase form to contrast with |
| Parallel runs without a repeated pattern | 210 | a shared first word alone is weak evidence |
| Borderline capitalisation | 35 | COVID and MIL are capitalised 80-89% of the time, which is weak contrast |
| List and layout runs | 18 | bullets and >> reference lists are layout, not rhetoric |
| Headings | 1 | formatting, not emphasis |
