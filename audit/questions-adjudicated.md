## Decision rules

**Missed candidates**
- `SEGMENTATION_ERROR` — ends on an abbreviation or lone initial, exceeds 160 characters, or has unbalanced brackets. The unit is a fragment, not a question.
- `ADD_Q_QUESTION` — Q wrote it verbatim and it either ends with `?` or is an information request whose wording is asked with `?` elsewhere in the corpus.
- `NEEDS_CONTEXT` — reads as a question but has neither a question mark nor corroboration. Bare `Define.` / `List.` land here deliberately.

**Stored, not in Q source**
- Content-word overlap against every line of the post locates the fragment a paraphrase was written from.
- `KEEP_AS_EDITORIAL_NORMALIZATION` — 60%+ overlap with one Q line. Both texts are kept with provenance; `neverDisplayAsQ: true`.
- `NEEDS_CONTEXT` — 34–59%. Could be a normalisation or a merge of several lines.
- `REMOVE_FROM_Q_QUESTIONS` — below 34%. No Q line accounts for it.

## Not touched

The 1,242 `STORED_PRESENT_SEGMENTATION_DIFF` records are Q's own words with a boundary drawn differently by two tools. They are excluded from this pass by design.
